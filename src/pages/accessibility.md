We want everyone to be able to enjoy crafting with Sparkle. This site aims to meet the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA**.

## What we do

- Colour contrast checked to WCAG AA, including our yellow buttons with charcoal text
- Body text is at least 16px and can be zoomed to 200% without breaking the page
- Every image has a text description (alt text)
- The whole site works with a keyboard, with clear focus outlines and a "skip to content" link
- Pages work with screen readers, using proper headings, labels and landmarks
- Animations are switched off if your device asks for reduced motion
- Videos only load when you choose to play them

## Known issues

- Some older printable PDFs aren't fully tagged for screen readers yet. Email us and we'll send an accessible version.

## Tell us

If something doesn't work for you, please email [hello@sparklebykimberley.co.uk](mailto:hello@sparklebykimberley.co.uk) and we'll do our best to fix it quickly.
