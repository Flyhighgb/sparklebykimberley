We keep things light. This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

## Essential storage (always on)

These are needed for the site to work, so they don't need your consent under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

| Name | Type | What it does | How long |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `sparkle-cart` | Local storage | Remembers what's in your basket | Until you clear it or check out |
| `sparkle-consent` | Local storage | Remembers your cookie choices | Until you change them |

When you check out, you're taken to Stripe's secure payment page, which sets its own essential cookies for fraud prevention. See [Stripe's cookie policy](https://stripe.com/gb/legal/cookies-policy).

## Optional (only with your OK)

| Service | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Cloudflare Web Analytics** | Counts page visits so we know which tutorials you love. It doesn't use cookies or follow you across other sites. |
| **YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode)** | Plays tutorial videos. YouTube may set cookies once a video is loaded. |

## Changing your mind

You can change your choices at any time using the **Cookie settings** link at the bottom of every page.
