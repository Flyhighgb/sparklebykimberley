Did you make these at school? I made hundreds! They're a brilliant family make because grown-ups can fold while kids dream up the fortunes.

**How to play:** Ask a friend to pick a colour and spell it out, opening and closing the fortune teller with each letter. Then they pick a number and you count it out. Finally they pick a number, you lift the flap and read their fortune!
